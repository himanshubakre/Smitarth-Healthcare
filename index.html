About SMITARTH HEALTHCARE

Care That Feels Personal

SMITARTH HEALTHCARE is run by a Consultant Cardiovascular and Respiratory Physiotherapist with 11 years of experience, right here in Beturkar Pada, Kalyan West.

No two patients are the same, so your treatment is planned around your pain, your condition, and your daily routine. This includes pain relief, pelvic floor rehabilitation, and support for cardiovascular and respiratory conditions.
